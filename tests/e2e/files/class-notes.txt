Channels and BOC levels: class notes for the end-to-end tests

A channel is the normal moving range of a stock, set by HG for each ticker.
Channel lines sit one channel apart above and below the last close.
The BOC level sits one BOC past each channel line, toward the next line.
A break of a level only counts when the stock closes below it, not when it wicks through it.
When a stock breaks below the break level, the play is the put at the put strike for the listed expiry.
The five day median is the midpoint between the week's highest high and lowest low.
Take profits at thirty percent instead of waiting for a perfect exit.
Never risk more than you can afford to lose on a single options trade.
